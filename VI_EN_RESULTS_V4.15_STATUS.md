# v4.15 status — validation gate failed; holdout sealed

v4.15 tests whether lowering the five latent-objective terms to multiplier 0.5 improves preservation relative to the v4.14 scale of 1.0, against a matched token-only control. The reviewed corpus uses a fresh split and holdout, 15,360 AI-authored synthetic records across 192 event combinations (8,960/3,200/3,200 train/validation/release holdout), four context-balanced surface forms, and a fixed workshop location. Its draft fingerprint is `8161a73afee325d47faf7756c7024b0dd0d62157d80da446e67d83506151c5b9`. Two independent `gpt-6-luna` high reviews and an artifact-bound AI adjudication approved the corpus for this preliminary experiment. Their exact reviewed-artifact bindings are private under ignored `data/`.

The protocol compared `token_only` with TIDE latent-objective multipliers 0.5 and 1.0, all at source-copy weight 1.5, over seeds 17/23/41. All nine CPU runs completed 29 epochs and 3,248 updates each, followed by validation-only generation. The frozen TIDE gate failed: only 3/60 per-seed bucket checks passed (2/30 at 0.5 and 1/30 at 1.0), all three on Vietnamese held-out paths. No single-action bucket passed preservation (0/48); three of twelve path buckets passed. Unicode and EOS were 100%. Preservation is the main blocker, with action fidelity also missing some buckets. The release holdout remains sealed with no test metrics or generation artifacts.

Pooled single-action preservation was 53.2% for TIDE ×0.5, 49.7% for TIDE ×1.0, and 61.0% for token-only. On paths, TIDE ×0.5 reached 64.2% English and 80.2% Vietnamese; ×1.0 reached 60.8% and 72.9%; token-only reached 74.8% and 75.0%. Each seed's TIDE validation token CE and path-token CE was also worse than its matched token-only control. Thus the tested lower dose gives only localized Vietnamese gains; it does not support a broad TIDE advantage. Seeds reuse the same 40 validation groups, so denominators are operational checks, not independent linguistic observations.

Aggregate-only diagnosis of the private validation outputs found missing patient phrases and required verb phrases to be the most frequent preservation failures. Missing agent phrases and the invariant workshop location were much less frequent. This diagnosis uses the pilot's narrow synthetic checker and does not measure naturalness. Detailed aggregate scores and per-seed denominators are in [VI_EN_RESULTS_V4.15_VALIDATION.md](VI_EN_RESULTS_V4.15_VALIDATION.md); the attribute-count table is included there without generated text.

Two independent Luna reviewers examined only the aggregate validation report. They agreed that preservation dominates, no single-action cell passes, Unicode/EOS pass, and no broad TIDE advantage is supported. One recommended another lower latent dose; the other noted prior source-copy dose tests and recommended that the next fresh pilot test still lower multipliers. The next registered hypothesis is a new v4.16 corpus/split/holdout with token-only versus TIDE multipliers 0.1 and 0.25, fixed source-copy weight 1.5, the same seeds/training protocol, and unchanged gates. This tests whether reducing auxiliary pressure further improves reconstruction and preservation; it is a hypothesis, not a presumed fix. v4.15 remains immutable and its holdout will not be used for tuning.

The duplicated path-transition representation is explicitly disclosed in the frozen data statement: 3,072 logical transitions appear in both standalone and path-linked roles across all splits (1,792 train, 640 validation, 640 release holdout). Only the 1,792 training copies increase training exposure; validation and holdout duplicates are evaluation records. This is identical across conditions and means the experiment studies a path-enriched distribution.

This is a preliminary synthetic AI-authored/AI-reviewed pilot, not human/native-speaker validation. PhoMT was not used; Phan Rang Cham remains excluded. Corpus/reviews/protocol and generated outputs remain local under Git-ignored `data/` and `runs/`.
