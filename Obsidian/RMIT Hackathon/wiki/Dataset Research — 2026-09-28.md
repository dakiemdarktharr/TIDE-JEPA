# Dataset research — 2026-09-28

> Scope: dataset and corpus evidence for TIDE-JEPA's current Vietnamese, English, and Phan Rang Cham scope. The user requires model/trainer/baseline code to be written from scratch; PyTorch is allowed. This is an evidence review, not a data-use decision. No dataset was downloaded, terms accepted, or data imported.

## Findings at a glance

- **No located resource is ready-made supervised action-conditioned generation data across all three target languages.** Existing bilingual corpora align sentences but do not carry the project's event-frame/action annotations. The Cham corpora/resources located either have a variety uncertainty or are only lexical/reference materials.
- **Phan Rang Cham remains the critical gap.** A 2026 corpus paper reports 11,481 Cham news documents, but calls the language only “Cham”; it does not establish that this is specifically Phan Rang/Eastern Cham. Its news and summary tasks are not aligned controlled-generation examples. Ask authors to identify the variety, orthography, data access, and underlying text terms before treating it as candidate Cham data.
- **For Vietnamese–English text, PhoMT and MTet are meaningful parallel-corpus candidates** for linguistic inspection or small-budget examples after the open data-use question is resolved. Neither provides action labels or a held-out-composition design. Their terms differ materially.
- **For action-label scaffolding, UD English EWT includes Tense and Polarity features; UD Vietnamese VTB is much smaller and its documented feature list is empty.** These are useful annotations/corpora to inspect, not equivalent cross-lingual event/action supervision.
- **The from-scratch-code constraint is compatible with public data in principle.** It bars code reuse; it does not itself bar using data. Data terms, derived annotations, copyright/source rights, and attribution/share-alike obligations still need independent review.

## Fit for typed meaning actions

**Confirmed:** PhoMT and MTet pair Vietnamese with English text, but the data fields shown are translations only. UD English EWT exposes Tense/Polarity feature annotations, while UD Vietnamese VTB reports no morphological feature inventory and contains negation-related syntax.

**Inference:** These resources may support sampling sentences and creating a separately documented, human-checked action/frame layer, but that would be new annotation, not inherited gold labels. Parallel translation pairs should not be assumed to preserve grammatical tense, polarity marking, or event structure one-to-one. For the action study, an in-house frame-to-realization set may be required; for Phan Rang Cham it would need knowledgeable speakers and language-specific validation before collection/design.

**Unknown:** No examined source provides consistent Vietnamese–English–Phan Rang Cham triples annotated for event/meaning frame, temporal anchoring, and polarity. No verified Cham system of tense/polarity labels or writing conventions applicable to TIDE-JEPA has been established here.

## Terms, code constraint, and community governance

- **Code:** Using these datasets does not violate the user's no-code-reuse instruction by itself. Implement all models, trainers, and project baselines from scratch. Dataset loading/conversion may use standard libraries, subject to terms.
- **PhoMT:** Gated research/education-only use and no redistribution of the dataset or modified data; publication citation required. Whether derived labels/material count as prohibited modified redistribution should be checked against the actual agreement.
- **MTet / UD:** Published dataset/treebank labels are CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 or CC BY-SA 4.0 respectively. Attribution and share-alike apply; NC applies to MTet. Treebank license declarations do not necessarily resolve rights in underlying source texts; UD itself cautions that some licenses cover annotations only, not underlying texts ([UD licensing guidance](https://universaldependencies.org/contributing/licensing.html)).
- **CKTN / Cham:** The arXiv paper's CC BY 4.0 license covers the paper. No distinct license for crawled corpus text was found. Sources are institutional news outlets, so web availability and inclusion in a research corpus should not be read as training/republication permission. No community consent, access-control, takedown, benefit-sharing, or governance mechanism is described in the passages inspected. This is an unknown, not evidence of absent consent.
- **Community fit:** CKTN reports unstable/mixed romanization and multiple scripts, and the ELP/ABVD sources establish language variety but not contemporary conventions. For Phan Rang Cham, a technically available Latin-script text corpus may represent outlet norms and language ideologies rather than community-preferred output. Appropriate speaker/community consultation is necessary before fixing actions, orthography, or release policy.

## Inference

1. The most promising existing Vietnamese–English parallel resources are PhoMT for quality-checked sentence pairs and MTet for scale/domain breadth. Neither is a direct TIDE-JEPA task dataset.
2. UD English EWT can help identify English tense/polarity examples; UD Vietnamese VTB can help locate negation constructions. Automatically reusing those feature tags as the project's cross-lingual meaning actions risks annotation mismatch.
3. CKTN merits author follow-up only if Cham variety is confirmed as Phan Rang Cham, and only after data/text rights and community conditions are known. Its documents alone do not instantiate the controlled generation task.
4. A dictionary/word list is valuable language infrastructure but cannot be scored as sentence-generation training data.

## Unknowns / follow-up evidence needed

- Does CKTN's Cham portion specifically represent Phan Rang/Eastern Cham (`cjm`), and what orthographic mixtures/normalization exist per document?
- Is CKTN data downloadable, and what terms apply to corpus text versus the paper and annotations? Are there consent/takedown arrangements with the Cham community and source publishers?
- PhoMT's agreement: how does it treat derived annotations, model weights, example excerpts, and project-specific transformed data?
- For MTet and the UD treebanks, are intended experimental uses covered by the declared license, including publication, commercialization possibility, and model output release? What rights attach to underlying texts?
- Do linguistic experts/speakers validate that the candidate action inventory (including PAST/NOW and positive/negative) captures appropriate realizations in each language, especially Vietnamese and Phan Rang Cham?
- What exact fixed budget, split protocol, and human review rubric will define the action-conditioned evaluation set?

### More evidence on variety, orthography, and governance

- The [Endangered Languages Project entry](https://elcat.colo.hawaii.edu/lang/4173?hl=en) explicitly equates Eastern Cham with Phan Rang Cham and gives ISO `cjm`; it identifies traditional Akhar Thrah and locates speakers mainly in Ninh Thuận. This is language metadata, not a data release.
- The Baclawski primary paper describes fieldwork with six native speakers from the villages near Phan Rang, task elicitation and consultant validation; it also reports broader variation and Vietnamese bilingualism ([paper](https://linguistics.berkeley.edu/~kbaclawski/Baclawski_2019_Cham-Wh.pdf)). The data may be good linguistic evidence for colloquial sentence forms, but its published examples cannot substitute for verified permission to use the archive data.
- EAP698's item-level descriptions connect the digitized material to named/private owners and Ninh Thuận originals. Some are literary and others religious/ritual. This supports provenance and sensitivity assessment; it does not establish that original contributors granted unrestricted ML consent. See [British Library catalogue records](https://searcharchives.bl.uk/?f%5Bcollection_area_ssi%5D%5B%5D=Endangered+Archives+Programme&f%5Blanguage_ssim%5D%5B%5D=Eastern+Cham&f%5Blevel_ssi%5D%5B%5D=%5B32%5D+Fonds&per_page=100&search_field=all_fields&sort=date).
- *Dictionnaire Cam-Vietnamien-Français* and the dated EAP manuscripts should not be called public domain based on age or online visibility. The sources inspected did not supply an explicit public-domain status or text/model-training license. Copyright status, ownership, reproduction permission and culturally appropriate use remain unverified.

### Inference

1. A precise Phan Rang variety is demonstrably documented in both linguistic field materials and Ninh Thuận manuscript collections. These are materially better leads than the CKTN “Cham” corpus, whose variety is unconfirmed.
2. CLA field materials may eventually support a small controlled-generation study if the archive custodians/contributors authorize access/use and its actual content has suitable frame-to-realization contrasts. The paper's acceptance/felicity tasks make action-oriented annotation conceivable, but do not prove that suitable action labels, parallel translations, or systematic polarity/temporal contrasts exist.
3. EAP698 material could support study of traditional written language/literature after transcription and governance review, but should not be conflated with contemporary colloquial Phan Rang Cham generation data.

### Unknowns / evidence still needed

- CLA's record-level inventory, media/transcript content, current archival (rather than prearchival) state, access controls, consent scope, license, and whether trained-model use is permitted.
- EAP698 item-level rights/reproduction license, permission from manuscript owners/communities for corpus/model use, and whether any transcripts or translations exist alongside image scans.
- Any openly downloadable, structured Phan Rang Cham text dataset on Hugging Face, Zenodo or a paper-linked repository. No such exact-variety dataset was confirmed in this search; absence from search is not proof none exists.
- Any existing Phan Rang Cham corpus with sentence-aligned English/Vietnamese and labels for meaning frames, tense/temporal reference and polarity. None was found in this search.

### Eligibility conclusion for controlled generation

**No source located currently meets the evidence threshold for a ready-to-use Phan Rang Cham generation corpus that is both verified as the exact variety and explicitly action/frame-labelled with confirmed usable terms.** CLA fieldwork is an exact-variety research-data lead with potentially relevant sentence/discourse judgments, but current access and terms are unverified. EAP698 is a confirmed exact-area written manuscript archive but image-based, culturally sensitive in parts, and research-only in the BL catalogue. ABVD and the 1971 dictionary are lexicons/reference works, not generation corpora. Therefore, none should currently be described as an approved or ready dataset for action-conditioned training.
