# Phan Rang Cham dataset follow-up

> Status: Source-grounded research summary reported by `find`, 2026-09-28. This is evidence for candidate discovery, not a data-use authorization.

## Finding

No located source currently meets all three requirements for TIDE-JEPA training: verified Phan Rang/Eastern Cham variety, sentence/frame/action-labelled controlled-generation material, and documented terms permitting the intended use. No data was downloaded or imported.

## Exact-variety leads

- **California Language Archive, Eastern Cham Field Materials (2014–20):** the collection is titled Eastern Cham. Kenneth Baclawski's [linguistics paper](https://linguistics.berkeley.edu/~kbaclawski/Baclawski_2019_Cham-Wh.pdf) describes native Eastern Cham consultants born and raised near Phan Rang and naturally elicited sentences/discourses with grammaticality/felicity judgments. The paper describes a tranche as prearchival; its inspected metadata do not establish current record inventory, access, license, or model-training permission. [CLA collection listing](https://cla.berkeley.edu/browse/collection-list.html); [researcher's archive citation](https://kenny.baclawski.org/index.php/papers/).
- **British Library EAP698:** catalogue entries identify Eastern Cham / Cham script and Ninh Thuận provenance, including [sample item EAP698/22](https://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/032-003319252). The catalogue describes research-purpose access; no reuse or training license was established. Items include manuscript images and some ritual/cultural material, so item-level permissions and community protocols matter. [Catalogue search](https://searcharchives.bl.uk/?f%5Bcollection_area_ssi%5D%5B%5D=Endangered+Archives+Programme&f%5Blanguage_ssim%5D%5B%5D=Eastern+Cham&f%5Blevel_ssi%5D%5B%5D=%5B32%5D+Fonds&per_page=100&search_field=all_fields&sort=date).
- **ABVD Phan Rang Cham:** exact-variety lexical resource with 201 entries, not a sentence-generation corpus. [Language entry](https://lpan.eva.mpg.de/austronesian/language.php?id=371).
- **Moussay, *Dictionnaire Cam-Vietnamien-Français* (1971):** bibliographic records place publication in Phan Rang; it is a dictionary, not action-conditioned sentence data. An online scan listing says licensing information is unspecified. [WorldCat record](https://search.worldcat.org/fr/title/dictionnaire-cam-vietnamien-francais-tu-ien-cham-viet-phap/oclc/937599775?ht=edition&referer=di); [library record](https://www.emiclib.com/Item/ItemDetail/282112); [scan listing and its licensing statement](https://www.nguoicham.com/document/382/cu%E1%BB%91n-t%E1%BB%B1-%C4%91i%E1%BB%87n-cham-vi%E1%BB%87t-ph%C3%A1p-c%E1%BB%A7a-cha-c%E1%BB%91-g%C3%A9rard-moussay-v%C3%A0-7-nh%C3%A2n-s%E1%BB%89-ch%C4%83m-so%E1%BA%A1/).

## Decision and follow-up

These sources establish promising research leads, not approved training data. Keep the Phan Rang Cham model registry entry without default grammar/action inventory. Before considering any source for use, establish exact variety and content, current access, rights and consent scope (including model use), and appropriate community/linguistic review. The full dated assessment, including item-level distinctions and unknowns, is in [[../wiki/Dataset Research — 2026-09-28]].
