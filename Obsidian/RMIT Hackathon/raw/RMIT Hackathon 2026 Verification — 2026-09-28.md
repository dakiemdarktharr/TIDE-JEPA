> Checked: 2026-09-28

## Facts on the current main page

- Four tasks are connected to Security, Generative AI, and Low-Resource Languages.
- The “Language” field says English; the page does not clarify whether this denotes task language, interface language, or data language.
- Platform: Kaggle, hosted by SkywardAI Labs.
- The four task details will be revealed on hackathon day.
- Every team member must attend the same venue; online or cross-venue attendance is not allowed. Each team picks one campus from Melbourne, Saigon South, Hanoi, or Taylor's University in Malaysia.
- Team size is four currently enrolled undergraduate or postgraduate students; one registration is submitted by the leader.
- Published schedule: 8am–6pm, 22 October 2026, then 8am–12pm, 23 October 2026. Published registration deadline: 15 October 2026.
- No exact task prompts, dataset details, scoring criteria, submission formats, or required pitch/demo/report are given on the page.

## Conflicts and limits

The [News & Updates page](https://rmit-hackathon.com/news-update.html) says the detailed brief and judging criteria remain to be confirmed, consistent with the main page withholding task details. It also says exact event dates are still being confirmed, conflicting with the main page's 22–23 October schedule. The main page says task details will appear on hackathon day; no specific scoring, metric, file, or presentation deliverables are published there. Record this internal site inconsistency and recheck official materials before relying on operational details.

The main-page “English” field does not establish that competitors may only use English model data, and it does not alter the TIDE-JEPA research language scope. No attempt was made to open a Kaggle event/competition page or contact the organizing committee.
